Version 01.01	15 minutes dev	2 minutes CPU	3 minutes test		20 minutes

I wanted to implement another Basic Computer Games app. The one I chose was
Trap. I liked the game, and it's not a variant you see very often.
To start off, as I worked on the initial design, I asked Claude if it had
access to the book, and if it could determine how it was played. He could.
Or rather, he said he could. Massive error in his version: selecting numbers
that trap a number wins. You could win every game by selecting 1 to 100. 
Obviously not good.
For the initial version, I got Claude to understand the game rules, and to 
give it classic style font: something from the 50's. It was a nice first
pass. As always, plenty of items to follow up with.


Version 01.02	10 minutes dev	3 minutes CPU	2 minutes test		35 minutes

The most significant defect was that there are times where you make a move
and no response is shown. It makes the game unplayable. I asked Claude to
resolve. The first attempt did not work. I showed Claude a screenshot to
make it more clear. That did result in the issue being found and fixed.
